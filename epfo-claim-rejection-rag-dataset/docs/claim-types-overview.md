# Claim types overview

Use this with the rejection records. Always re-check the live [Which Claim Form](https://www.epfindia.gov.in/site_en/WhichClaimForm.php?id=in_sm) page and the member portal dropdown.

## Form 19 — PF final settlement
- **What**: Employee share + employer share + interest of EPF (not the EPS pension corpus).
- **Typical eligibility**: Left covered employment / retired; DOE on record; historically **not before two months** after leaving (OCS FAQ); not presently in a covered establishment.
- **Online needs**: Activated UAN, Aadhaar e-KYC OTP, bank+IFSC seeded, PAN if service < 5 years.
- **Frequent rejects**: DOE missing (epfo-rr-024), still employed (034), waiting period (033), name/KYC (001–010), bank (015–022), wrong form (043).
- **2026 note**: Secondary sources describe 75% access after a shorter unemployment period and 25% retention on partials; Mint 3 Jul 2026 still cited 60 days for Form 19. Portal wins.

## Form 31 — Partial withdrawal / advance
- **What**: Non-refundable advance while in service (and some special circumstances).
- **Needs**: DOJ in database (OCS FAQ). Purpose must be one the portal enables.
- **2026 reporting**: Categories grouped as Essential Needs, Housing Needs, Special Circumstances; uniform **12 months** membership; retain **25%**.
- **Frequent rejects**: Wrong purpose (039), amount cap (040), DOJ missing (025), KYC/bank, still using 31 as fake 19 (034).
- **Purpose-specific rows**: housing purchase/construction (082), loan repayment (083), renovation (084), plot/site (131), marriage (085), education (086), medical (087/094/129), calamity (088), COVID legacy (089), unemployment/non-contribution (090/125), cut in wages (091), factory closure (092), handicapped equipment (093), pre-retirement 90% (095), insufficient service remark (096), special circumstances misuse (121).

## Form 10C — EPS withdrawal benefit / scheme certificate
- **Cash withdrawal**: Total EPS service **> 6 months and < 9.5 years** (OCS FAQ Q4) plus Form 19-style exit conditions.
- **>= 10 years (9.5 rounded)**: Do not cash out; take Scheme Certificate or later Form 10D (036).
- **< 6 months**: Skip 10C; Form 19 only (035).
- **Frequent rejects**: Wrong service band, Form 11 EPS mismatch (044), missing EPS contributions (042).

## Form 10D — Monthly pension
- **What**: Lifetime EPS pension; family/widow/disablement variants.
- **Needs**: Generally **10 years** service and age **58** (or **50–57** reduced). Disablement exception exists.
- **Frequent rejects**: Service short because of gaps (037), age/DOB (038, 002), family details (064), bank not single/spouse-joint (023), photos/descriptive roll (081).

## Form 13 — Transfer
- Moves old member ID into current UAN.
- Needs employer DSC/e-sign (previous or present). 15-day printout rule may still apply in some flows (032).
- **Overlap**: Source office should **not** reject solely for genuine overlap (circular 20 May 2025) (070, 041).
- **Frequent rejects**: Employer not attesting (068), name mismatch source vs destination (071), Annexure K missing (069).

## Form 20 — PF on death
- Nominee / family / legal heir claims EPF accumulations of the deceased.
- **Frequent rejects**: No valid e-signed nomination (056, 060), wrong claimant (061), death certificate (062), succession (063), deceased name mismatch (066).

## Form 5IF — EDLI
- Insurance **only if death occurred while in service** (official instruction) (067).
- Documents: death certificate, cancelled cheque of claimant, guardianship/succession as applicable; extra 12-month PF details if exempted trust (031).
- Weekend/holiday between two jobs: EPFO has clarified not to treat as a break (ET reporting).

## Composite Claim Form
- **Aadhaar CCF**: UAN activated, Aadhaar+bank seeded; **no employer attestation**.
- **Non-Aadhaar CCF**: Employer attestation mandatory.
- Online member-portal claim is the digital equivalent of CCF for 19/31/10C.

## UMANG / member portal online claims
- Same eligibility engine. UMANG is a front end (055). Prefer portal for messy service-history cases.
- OTP must be the **UIDAI** mobile (008, 054).

## Death package (typical)
- In service, before/after 58, with/without 10 years: see Which Claim Form. Usually **20 + 10D or 10C + 5IF** when in service; **no 5IF** when death away from service.

## International workers
- UAN still required. Specified classes may file **physical claims without Aadhaar seeding** (circular 29.11.2024) with passport and bank verification; employer identity confirm if balance > Rs 5 lakh (074).
- CoC/SSA is a separate IW-portal product (075).

## Inoperative accounts
- Unblock after KYC; extra officer layers for flagged high-value dormant MIDs (045, 076). Do not expect a normal 3-day auto-settle.
