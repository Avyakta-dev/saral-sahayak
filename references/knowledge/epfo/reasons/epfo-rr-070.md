# Transfer claim rejected solely due to overlapping service (should generally be processed after 20 May 2025 circular) (epfo-rr-070)

> Dataset record `epfo-rr-070`. This educational record is not official EPFO guidance or legal advice. Conversion preserves the archived source; it does not independently verify current policy.

## Rejection phrase and aliases

**Canonical phrase:** Transfer claim rejected solely due to overlapping service (should generally be processed after 20 May 2025 circular)

**Aliases:** Transfer rejected for overlap, Overlap per-se disqualification, Form 13 overlap rejection, Transfer rejected due to overlap, Service overlapping Form 13, Genuine overlap should not reject transfer

## Classification

- **Category:** Transfer_Related
- **Affected claim types:** Form 13 Transfer
- **Severity:** medium
- **Official/common message status:** Circular WSU/TransferClaim/E-52972/2025-26/07 dated 20/May/2025: transfer requests are being rejected due to overlap; however overlapping services can occur for genuine reasons and should not be a disqualification per se. Transferor (source) office must process even with overlapping services; only if a genuine need is felt should clarification be obtained. Also reiterates source-office duty to verify details (ref WSU circular 25/04/2025).

## What it means

If a Form 13 is still rejected in 2026 only for overlap, that may be non-compliance with HO instructions. Members should resubmit and quote the circular, while still correcting obviously wrong dates. This record is separate from epfo-rr-041 because the legal instruction is transfer-specific.

## Root cause

Field office using old SOP; residual system flag; genuine fraud-like dual employment that does need clarification.

## How it is detected

Transfer remark cites overlap only. Dates intersect.

## Fix

- Download the 20 May 2025 circular text and attach it to EPFiGMS / a letter to the source Regional Office.
- Provide appointment and relieving letters explaining the overlap (notice period, weekend, contractor switch).
- If dates are simply wrong, correct them rather than arguing.
- Ask the transferor office to process as instructed; do not accept overlap-only rejection as final.

## Required documents

- Circular WSU/TransferClaim/E-52972/2025-26/07
- Both employment letters
- Form 13 claim ID

## Who acts

mixed

## Prevention

- Keep joining/relieving letters for every job switch.
