# Form 31 home renovation/alteration/improvement advance rejected (epfo-rr-084)

> Dataset record `epfo-rr-084`. This educational record is not official EPFO guidance or legal advice. Conversion preserves the archived source; it does not independently verify current policy.

## Rejection phrase and aliases

**Canonical phrase:** Form 31 home renovation/alteration/improvement advance rejected

**Aliases:** House renovation Form 31 rejected, Addition alteration advance not allowed, Repair of dwelling house rejected, Improvement of house PF advance

## Classification

- **Category:** Eligibility_Service
- **Affected claim types:** Form 31 Partial Withdrawal/Advance, Composite Claim Form, UMANG/Member Portal Online Claim
- **Severity:** medium
- **Official/common message status:** ClearTax/Form 31 guides: addition/alteration/improvement historically after house completion with caps around 12x wages or employee share/cost, and limits on frequency. Property must be member/spouse/joint. CCF circular dispensed with utilization certificates for several housing heads when self-certifying.

## What it means

Renovation is not purchase. Rejection when ownership ineligible, waiting period since completion/prior advance unmet, amount exceeds renovation cap, or permitted renovation advances exhausted.

## Root cause

Wrong sub-purpose; ownership; frequency/waiting rules; over-claim.

## How it is detected

Purpose renovation/alteration. Service and prior-advance history.

## Fix

- Confirm house registered in member/spouse/joint name.
- Use renovation purpose, not fresh purchase.
- Respect amount and frequency limits on portal.
- If ineligible, use another purpose only if truly applicable.

## Required documents

- Ownership proof if asked
- Self-declaration
- Prior advance history

## Who acts

member

## Prevention

- Track prior housing advances before renovation filing.
