# EDLI denied or delayed for contribution continuity / break-in-service beyond clarified weekend-holiday gaps (epfo-rr-139)

> Dataset record `epfo-rr-139`. This educational record is not official EPFO guidance or legal advice. Conversion preserves the archived source; it does not independently verify current policy.

## Rejection phrase and aliases

**Canonical phrase:** EDLI denied or delayed for contribution continuity / break-in-service beyond clarified weekend-holiday gaps

**Aliases:** EDLI break in service, Contribution discontinuity Form 5IF, Gap between jobs EDLI rejected, Not continuous member EDLI, EDLI gap between employments

## Classification

- **Category:** Nominee_Death
- **Affected claim types:** Form 5IF EDLI Death Insurance
- **Severity:** high
- **Official/common message status:** EPFO has clarified that weekends/holidays between two jobs are not a break for EDLI. Longer uncredited gaps, missing ECR months, or exits without rejoining can still defeat death-while-in-service or continuity tests. Secondary compliance notes discuss short-gap regulation — verify live circular text; do not invent numbers.

## What it means

Families lose EDLI when the member had resigned and not rejoined, or when multi-week/month gaps lack contributions, even if PF balance exists. Weekend-only gaps should not reject; longer breaks need RO review against current clarifications.

## Root cause

DOE without subsequent joining; missing ECR; treating any gap as fatal; or conversely ignoring a real non-employment period.

## How it is detected

Service History gap analysis. Form 5IF instruction died while in service. RO continuity worksheet.

## Fix

- Map exact DOE and next DOJ; note weekends/holidays vs calendar gaps.
- If gap is only intervening holidays, cite ET-reported EPFO clarification and ask RO to review.
- If member had truly exited without covered employment at death, EDLI is generally not payable (see 067) — pursue Form 20/10C-10D only.
- Supply joining letters and ECR to prove continuity when jobs chained closely.
- EPFiGMS with timeline chart if RO applied weekend-gap wrongly.

## Required documents

- Service History
- Relieving and joining letters
- ECR extracts bridging the gap
- Death certificate

## Who acts

mixed

## Prevention

- Employers must mark accurate DOE/DOJ; members should verify Service History after each job change.
