# High-value claim subjected to additional scrutiny or officer-layer hold (epfo-rr-163)

> Dataset record `epfo-rr-163`. This educational record is not official EPFO guidance or legal advice. Conversion preserves the archived source; it does not independently verify current policy.

## Rejection phrase and aliases

**Canonical phrase:** High-value claim subjected to additional scrutiny or officer-layer hold

**Aliases:** High value claim scrutiny, Additional verification large PF balance, Claim above threshold pending approval, Four eye check PF settlement, High value death claim extra documents

## Classification

- **Category:** Compliance_Legal
- **Affected claim types:** Form 19 PF Final Settlement, Form 20 Death PF Settlement, Form 5IF EDLI Death Insurance, Form 10D Monthly Pension, International Worker Claim, UMANG/Member Portal Online Claim
- **Severity:** high
- **Official/common message status:** Inoperative/high-risk SOPs and IW physical-claim clarifications describe extra verification for higher balances (e.g. employer identity confirm above stated rupee thresholds in Aadhaar-relaxation notes). Claims appear stuck Under process or returned for extra KYC rather than hard-rejected.

## What it means

Not automatic denial — elevated due diligence. Members mistaking delay for rejection refile harmfully. Related to inoperative scrutiny (076) but applies to active high balances too.

## Root cause

Internal monetary thresholds; fraud risk scoring; death-claim fraud controls.

## How it is detected

Long pending on large passbook. RO request for extra ID/employer confirm. EPFiGMS mentioning verification.

## Fix

- Respond completely to extra document requests once — identity, employment, bank.
- Do not file duplicate claims.
- For IW high balance, ensure employer confirmation letters as circular requires.
- EPFiGMS politely asking for checklist and timeline after reasonable wait.

## Required documents

- Extra identity proofs as listed by RO
- Employer confirmation if asked
- Bank proofs

## Who acts

mixed

## Prevention

- Keep KYC pristine before large settlements.
- E-sign nomination early for death cases.
