# Form 31 natural calamity / abnormal conditions advance rejected (epfo-rr-088)

> Dataset record `epfo-rr-088`. This educational record is not official EPFO guidance or legal advice. Conversion preserves the archived source; it does not independently verify current policy.

## Rejection phrase and aliases

**Canonical phrase:** Form 31 natural calamity / abnormal conditions advance rejected

**Aliases:** Natural calamity advance rejected, Flood earthquake PF advance, Abnormal conditions Form 31 rejected, Calamity affected area advance not allowed

## Classification

- **Category:** Eligibility_Service
- **Affected claim types:** Form 31 Partial Withdrawal/Advance, Composite Claim Form, UMANG/Member Portal Online Claim
- **Severity:** medium
- **Official/common message status:** EPFO COVID FAQ explicitly distinguishes natural-calamity purpose from pandemic purpose. Form 31 guides list advances when the member establishment/area is notified as calamity-affected. KnowMoney places calamity under Special Circumstances in 2026 explainers.

## What it means

Calamity advances fail when the member area/establishment is not covered by the applicable notification, the wrong purpose (e.g. COVID vs calamity) was chosen, or amount/balance rules fail. Official COVID FAQ: applying under Natural Calamity does NOT equal pandemic advance.

## Root cause

Wrong purpose vs notification; area not notified; amount breach.

## How it is detected

Purpose natural calamity. RO checks notification applicability.

## Fix

- Use Natural Calamity only if your case matches that head.
- Do not substitute COVID purpose for calamity or vice versa.
- Follow Special Circumstances instructions if portal merged heads.
- If rejected, check whether another Essential Need truly applies.

## Required documents

- Self-declaration
- Calamity notification reference if asked
- Address proof if asked

## Who acts

member

## Prevention

- Read purpose help text; calamity is not pandemic or unemployment.
